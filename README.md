Overview:
SouqFlow is a sales and inventory management platform designed for small beauty and cosmetics sellers in Lebanon. The platform helps merchants manage products, inventory, customer orders, quotes, invoices, customer balances, and manually recorded payments from a single dashboard.
Problem:
Many small beauty and cosmetics sellers currently manage inventory, orders, and customer balances using WhatsApp, spreadsheets, notebooks, and manual records. This leads to inventory errors, lost sales, unpaid balances, and time-consuming administrative work.
MVP Scope-Features:
-Product Management
-Inventory Management
-Low-Stock Alerts
-Customer Order Tracking
-Quote Generation
-Invoice Generation
-Customer Balance Tracking
-Manual Payment Recording
